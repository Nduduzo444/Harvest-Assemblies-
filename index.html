About Our Church

Harvest Assemblies of Christ Global Church is more than a place of worship — it is a spiritual home, a training ground for leaders, and a beacon of hope in the community. From our humble beginnings in Palmridge, Katlehong, we have grown into a Christ-centered community dedicated to worship, discipleship, and outreach.
